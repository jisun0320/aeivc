Mission

Opening a path from Asia to the European stage

AEIVC exists to discover outstanding classical singers from China and across Asia, and to connect them directly with European orchestras, conductors and audiences.

We believe that a competition should lead to a stage. That is why every round of AEIVC ends with a Winners' Concert in Europe — in Italy, the birthplace of Opera, and in the Netherlands, at one of the world's most celebrated concert halls.

01

Discover

Find exceptional voices through an accessible online video competition with no age limit.

02

Connect

Link Asian singers with European orchestras, conductors and concert halls.

03

Perform

Give every winner a real performance with orchestra, before a European audience.

Regulations · Voice

Competition Regulations

By submitting an application, the applicant agrees to all of the following Regulations. The Regulations and the FAQ apply together.

Article 1

General

  1. The Asia-Europe International Vocal Competition (hereafter "AEIVC" or "the Competition") is an international online video competition for classical singers.
  2. The Competition is held in two rounds, Round I and Round II. Each round is an independent competition with its own application period, jury evaluation, results and Winners' Concert.
  3. The official language of the Competition is English. In the event of any discrepancy between translations, the English version shall prevail.
Article 2

Eligibility

  1. The Competition is open to classical singers of all nationalities and all voice types, with a particular focus on singers from China and the wider Asian region.
  2. There is no age limit. All applicants are judged by the same artistic standard, without age divisions.
  3. Applicants may apply to Round I, Round II, or both. Each round requires a separate application and a separate entry fee.
Article 3

Application

  1. Applications are made only through the official online application form on www.aeivc.com, within the application period of each round:
    • Round I: 4 January 2027 – 28 February 2027, 23:59 Beijing Time (UTC+8)
    • Round II: 3 May 2027 – 27 June 2027, 23:59 Beijing Time (UTC+8)
  2. An application is valid only when the application form has been completed and the entry fee payment has been confirmed. Forms without confirmed payment are not considered received.
  3. The applicant's name must be written in English exactly as it appears in the passport. If the entry fee is paid by another person (for example, a parent), the payer's name must be entered in the designated field of the form.
  4. A confirmation email is sent after payment. If it is not received, the applicant should first check the spam folder and then contact the Organizer.
  5. Submitted applications cannot be changed. If duplicate applications are received, only the first submission will be evaluated.
Article 4

Entry Fee & Payment

  1. The entry fee and its periods (early / regular) will be announced before applications open.
  2. The entry fee is paid online by PayPal, Alipay or WeChat Pay, as indicated on the application form.
  3. Entry fees are non-refundable after payment, except when the Competition is cancelled by the Organizer.
  4. Any transfer or transaction fees are borne by the applicant.
Article 5

Repertoire

  1. Applicants perform two (2) Opera arias of their own choice.
  2. The two arias must be taken from different Operas by different composers.
  3. Each aria must be sung in its original language.
  4. Each aria should, in principle, be sung in its original key.
  5. Each aria must be performed complete. No cuts, omissions or edits are permitted.
  6. The total performance time must not exceed twenty (20) minutes.
  7. Accompaniment must be live piano or live orchestra. Pre-recorded backing tracks are not permitted. Applicants arrange their own accompanist; the accompanist is not evaluated.
Article 6

Video Requirements

  1. The performance must be submitted as one continuous, unedited video recording.
  2. The video must have been recorded on or after 1 July 2026. Recordings of past performances, including live concerts, are accepted if they meet this date and all other requirements.
  3. The camera must remain fixed throughout. Moving the camera or operating the zoom during the performance is not permitted.
  4. The singer's face and both hands must be clearly visible throughout the performance.
  5. Picture and sound must be clear enough for the jury to evaluate the performance and identify the applicant. Recordings made with a smartphone are accepted.
  6. Minor adjustments of brightness, contrast or overall audio level are permitted, provided they do not alter the performance. Combining takes, cutting within the performance and any audio correction are not permitted.
  7. If the recording exceeds twenty (20) minutes, it may be trimmed only after the 20-minute mark. Everything before that point must remain unedited.
  8. AI-generated performances, deepfakes, synthesized audio and any technology that materially alters the performance are strictly prohibited. The Organizer may request verification if the authenticity of a video is in question.
  9. The video must be uploaded to Google Drive and named with the round prefix R1 (Round I) or R2 (Round II), in the format R1_Voice_LastName_FirstName_Country.mp4 (e.g. R1_Voice_Wang_Li_China.mp4). Sharing must be set to "Anyone with the link can view" until the results are announced.
  10. Applicants in regions where Google services are not accessible (including mainland China) should contact the Organizer by email for an alternative video submission method.
Article 7

Jury & Evaluation

  1. Videos are evaluated by an international jury under the General Director, Prof. Inhye Kim.
  2. The jury evaluates musicality, vocal technique, interpretation, expression and overall artistry.
  3. All applicants are judged by the same standard, without age divisions.
  4. The decisions of the jury are final. Appeals are not accepted.
Article 8

Results

  1. Results are announced separately for each round: Round I on 26 March 2027, Round II on 30 July 2027.
  2. Results are published on the Winners page of www.aeivc.com and communicated to applicants by email.
Article 9

Prizes & Winners' Concerts

  1. Round I — the 1st Prize and 2nd Prize winners perform at the Winners' Concert on 2 June 2027 at PalaSole, Bellano (Lake Como), Italy, with the Lake Como Philharmonic Orchestra conducted by Roberto Gianola. Programme: Arias.
  2. Round II — the 1st Prize winner performs on 22 October 2027 at the Scheldetheater, Terneuzen, and on 23 October 2027 at the Concertgebouw, Amsterdam, with the Sofia Philharmonic Orchestra conducted by Nayden Todorov. Programme to be announced.
  3. All prize winners receive an official certificate and a video of their concert for personal reference.
  4. Further winner support (such as travel and accommodation) will be announced. Benefits may vary according to local conditions.
  5. The concert programme may be changed only with the agreement of the Organizer.
Article 10

Winner Obligations

  1. Winners must submit the following documents when requested by the Organizer (not at the application stage):
    • Copy of passport or ID
    • Biography of 100–150 words
    • Profile photo
    • Performance programme
    • Proof of age, if required
Article 11

Disqualification

  1. An application may be disqualified for false information, violation of the video requirements, misconduct, copyright infringement or any other violation of these Regulations.
Article 12

Copyright & Personal Data

  1. Applicants agree that their submitted videos and photographs may be used by the Organizer to promote the Competition.
  2. Copyright in the performed works remains with the respective rights holders.
  3. Personal data is used solely for the administration of the Competition.
Article 13

Alternative Application

  1. Applicants who are unable to use the online form or the online payment methods may request a written application form and bank transfer details by email. International transfer fees are borne by the applicant.
  2. Written applications are subject to the same deadline. Applicants should contact the Organizer at least one to two weeks before the deadline.
Article 14

Contact

  1. All enquiries should be sent to aeivc.official@gmail.com.
Competition Repertoire · Voice

Two Opera arias

Applicants perform two Opera arias of their own choice, in one continuous video of no more than 20 minutes.

Repertoire

Required

  • Two Opera arias of your choice
  • From different Operas, by different composers
  • Sung in the original language
  • In principle, in the original key
  • Each aria sung complete (no cuts or omissions)
  • Total time: maximum 20 minutes
  • Live piano or live orchestra accompaniment
Repertoire

Not permitted

  • Two arias from the same Opera or the same composer
  • Translations into another language
  • Any cut or omission within an aria
  • Pre-recorded backing tracks
Video

Recording your video

01

One take

One continuous, unedited recording containing both arias, recorded on or after 1 July 2026.

02

Fixed camera

No camera movement and no zoom. Your face and both hands must be visible throughout.

03

Clear sound

Picture and sound must be clear. A smartphone is fine — clarity matters more than the equipment.

04

Light edits only

Brightness, contrast and overall level may be adjusted. No combining takes, no cuts, no audio correction.

05

No AI

AI-generated or synthesized performances and deepfakes are strictly prohibited.

06

Upload & share

Upload to Google Drive as R1_Voice_LastName_FirstName_Country.mp4 (Round I) or R2_Voice_… (Round II) and set sharing to "Anyone with the link can view".

Applicants in mainland China. Google services may not be accessible. Please contact aeivc.official@gmail.com for an alternative video submission method.
